EnergyRank Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

EnergyRank ("the app") lets you rate and log energy drinks and share your ratings with friends. This policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and password (passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never visible to us in plain text).
  • Profile information: your username, display name, and optional profile photo.
  • Content you create: your drink ratings, notes, rating photos, drink logs, and daily limits you set.
  • Social activity: friend requests and friendships, likes on ratings, notifications, and users you block.
  • Reports: if you report a user, we store the report reason and the accounts involved.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or advertising identifiers, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

How we use your information

  • To create and run your account and show your profile, ratings, and activity to the people it is shared with.
  • To power features such as friends, leaderboards, notifications, and drink tracking.
  • To keep the community safe, including reviewing reports and enforcing our Terms of Use.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see your information

Your username, display name, profile photo, and ratings may be visible to other EnergyRank users. Blocking a user hides you from them in search and removes any friendship.

Service providers

We use Supabase to provide authentication, database, and file storage. Your data is stored and processed on their infrastructure on our behalf.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in the app under Settings, which removes your account and associated data. You can also contact us at the email below to request deletion.

Children

EnergyRank is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Health notice

Nutrition and caffeine information in the app is an estimate for tracking and fun. It is not medical advice.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
